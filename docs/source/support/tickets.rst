Accessing and interacting with support tickets
==============================================

If you raised a ticket without signing in (because you couldn't sign in or
sign up, or you reported a website problem while signed out), you receive
updates by email. Reply to the email to add a comment to the ticket.

Viewing your tickets
--------------------

1. Click on **Support** on the top navigation bar and then click on **View
   support tickets**:

   .. image:: tickets-1.png
       :scale: 50 %
       :alt: Support menu with View support tickets highlighted

2. The list shows open tickets that you raised or that have been shared with
   you. To see other tickets, change **Open Requests** to **Closed
   Requests** or **Open and Closed Requests**:

   .. image:: tickets-2.png
       :scale: 50 %
       :alt: Tickets list with the Open and Closed Requests option highlighted

   You can also show only one request type, or search with **Find
   tickets**.

3. Click on a ticket's **Reference** or **Summary** to open it:

   .. image:: tickets-3.png
       :scale: 50 %
       :alt: Tickets list with a ticket reference highlighted

Working with a ticket
---------------------

From a ticket you can:

* Click on **Show details** to see everything that was provided when the
  ticket was raised:

  .. image:: tickets-4.png
      :scale: 50 %
      :alt: Ticket page with Show details highlighted

* Add a comment or attachment in **Activity**, then click on **Post**.
* Change the ticket's status, if any options are listed under **Status**
  (for example "Resolve this issue"). Click on the option and confirm it in
  the dialog that opens. Which options appear depends on the ticket's
  current status; a resolved ticket may have none.
* Use **Grant Support Access** under **Support Data Access** to let Linaro
  Support see the data in your subscription while they work on the ticket.
  Once access has been granted, you can revoke it from the same place.
* Share the ticket with someone else: click on **Share**, enter their email
  address and click on **Add**:

  .. image:: tickets-5.png
      :scale: 50 %
      :alt: Ticket page with the Share email address box highlighted

  They are told by email that they have been added. They don't need an
  account already, but to see the ticket in the Hub they must
  :doc:`sign up </using/signup>` with the same email address.

To give people access to every ticket for a subscription, rather than one at
a time, see :doc:`/submgmt/support-shared-access`.
