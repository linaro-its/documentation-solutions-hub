User Settings
=============

The **User Settings** page shows the details of your Linaro Solutions Hub
account and lets you manage the things that belong to you, such as your
profile and your access tokens.

To open it:

1. Click on your name in the top-right of the page:

   .. image:: user-settings-1.png
       :scale: 50 %
       :alt: Top navigation bar with the user name button highlighted

2. Click on **User Settings**:

   .. image:: user-settings-2.png
       :scale: 50 %
       :alt: User menu with User Settings highlighted

The page opens on the **Profile** tab. The other tabs, and the separate
**Managed Accounts** page for service accounts you manage, are described in
the following pages:
