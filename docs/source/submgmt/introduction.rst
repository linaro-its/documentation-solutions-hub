Introduction
============

As a subscription administrator, you can:

* give people or groups a *seat* on the subscription, and remove it again
* add or remove the *permissions* in someone's seat
* see who has which seats
* change whether the subscription renews automatically, and who its contacts
  are
* choose who can see and comment on the subscription's support tickets

Seats and permissions
---------------------

A subscription's plan offers one or more *seat types*, such as **Subscription
Administrator**. Giving someone a seat gives them the permissions that come
with that seat type. Each seat can then be adjusted: you can remove
permissions the person doesn't need, or add back ones that were removed.

You can give a seat to a group instead of a single person. Everyone in the
group then has the seat for as long as they are a member.

A plan may limit how many seats of each type can be given out. The limit
appears next to the seat type when you assign a seat, for example
**Subscription Administrator (2/∞)** means two seats are in use and there is
no limit.

See the :doc:`glossary </glossary/index>` for more about seats, permissions
and plans.

Using the SPIRE CLI
-------------------

Everything on the **Access Management** page can also be done with the SPIRE
CLI. See :doc:`/cli/seat` for assigning and removing seats and changing their
permissions, and :doc:`/cli/sub` for reviewing a subscription's seats.
