Subscription Administration
===========================

If you are an administrator for a subscription, the **Subscription
Administration** area of the Linaro Solutions Hub lets you decide who can use
that subscription, keep its details up to date and choose who can see its
support tickets.

You will only see your subscriptions here if you hold a **Subscription
Administrator** seat on them. If you think you should have one, ask an existing
administrator of the subscription.

To open Subscription Administration:

1. Click on your name in the top-right of the page and then click on
   **Administration**.

2. Under **Subscription Administration**, click on **Go to Subscriptions**:

   .. image:: submgmt-1.png
       :scale: 50 %
       :alt: Administration page with the Go to Subscriptions button highlighted

3. Click on the subscription you want to manage:

   .. image:: submgmt-2.png
       :scale: 50 %
       :alt: Subscription Administration page with a subscription highlighted

The subscription opens on its **Access Management** page. The menu on the left
takes you to each part of Subscription Administration.

To manage the people and groups in your organisation, see
:doc:`/admin/index`. You can also manage seats with the SPIRE CLI; see
:doc:`/cli/seat` and :doc:`/cli/sub`.
